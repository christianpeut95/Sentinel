# Add or edit Symptom Data for a Case

*10 steps · Created Oct 14, 2026 · Source: demo.sentinelsurveillance.app*

---

## Step 01: Open a case record

![Step 01](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-01.webp)

## Step 02: Under symptoms click 'Edit' or 'Add Symptoms'

![Step 02](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-02.webp)

## Step 03: Scroll down to the Symptoms panel

![Step 03](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-03.webp)

## Step 04: Select a symptom to add

![Step 04](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-04.webp)

## Step 05: Click Add Onset Dates & Details

![Step 05](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-05.webp)

## Step 06: Add the date of onset if known

![Step 06](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-06.webp)

## Step 07: To add other symptoms not in the list Search for additional symptoms - these can be configured in settings

![Step 07](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-07.webp)

## Step 08: Add the onset date for the additional symptom

![Step 08](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-08.webp)

## Step 09: Click Save Changes

![Step 09](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-09.webp)

## Step 10: Note the date of onset has automatically updated to the earliest symptom onset date

![Step 10](images/add-or-edit-symptom-data-for-a-case/step-10.webp)
