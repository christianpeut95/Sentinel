# Creating a Case of a disease in Sentinel

This requires the database to have patients entered, and at least 1 disease entered

*15 steps · Created Oct 14, 2026 · Source: demo.sentinelsurveillance.app*

---

## Step 01: Click link "New Case"

![Step 01](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-01.webp)

## Step 02: Search for the patient or create a new patient if the patient does not exist

![Step 02](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-02.webp)

## Step 03: Review the patient details and update them if required before proceeding

![Step 03](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-03.webp)

## Step 04: Click Next

![Step 04](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-04.webp)

## Step 05: Click Type to search for diseases...

![Step 05](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-05.webp)

## Step 06: Enter the particular disease this is a case of

![Step 06](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-06.webp)

## Step 07: Select a confirmation status

![Step 07](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-07.webp)

## Step 08: Enter a date of onset if known

![Step 08](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-08.webp)

## Step 09: Click Next

![Step 09](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-09.webp)

## Step 10: Optionally you can add laboratory results at the same time

![Step 10](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-10.webp)

## Step 11: You can optionally add exposure information at the same time

![Step 11](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-11.webp)

## Step 12: Click Next

![Step 12](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-12.webp)

## Step 13: Review the case details

![Step 13](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-13.webp)

## Step 14: Click Create Case

![Step 14](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-14.webp)

## Step 15: You are now displayed with the new case

![Step 15](images/creating-a-case-of-a-disease-in-sentinel/step-15.webp)
