# Assign an existing custom field to a disease

How to use an existing custom field in Sentinel and assign it to a disease in order for it to be collected for cases of that disease

*11 steps · Created Oct 13, 2026 · Source: demo.sentinelsurveillance.app*

---

## Step 01: Click link "Settings →"

![Step 01](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-01.webp)

## Step 02: Click link "Disease & Case Management"

![Step 02](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-02.webp)

## Step 03: Click link "Diseases"

![Step 03](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-03.webp)

## Step 04: Click the edit icon on your selected disease

![Step 04](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-04.webp)

## Step 05: Click Custom Fields

![Step 05](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-05.webp)

## Step 06: Check your custom field

![Step 06](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-06.webp)

## Step 07: Optional: Toggle whether this custom field will inherit to all child diseases

![Step 07](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-07.webp)

## Step 08: Click Save Custom Fields

![Step 08](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-08.webp)

## Step 09: Navigate to a case of your modified disease

![Step 09](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-09.webp)

## Step 10: Open the relevant custom field area

![Step 10](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-10.webp)

## Step 11: Confirm the custom field appears

![Step 11](images/assign-an-existing-custom-field-to-a-disease/step-11.webp)
